Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we handle website performance monitoring and technical data with complete transparency.

1. Information We Collect

Magnific Coupons is designed as a privacy-friendly deals directory. We do not require user registration, account creation, or submission of personal identity information to access our promo codes.

When you browse our website, standard non-identifying technical data (such as browser type, operating system, and page access timestamps) is automatically logged by web servers for security and functionality.

2. Webmaster Tools & Analytics (Google Search Console)

We utilize Google Search Console exclusively for webmaster monitoring, search indexing, identifying crawl errors, and ensuring overall technical health of our website.

Google Search Console does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) from individual site visitors.

3. Third-Party Referral Links & Cookies

When you click an affiliate or deal button (such as "Show Coupon" or "Claim Deal"), you will be redirected to the vendor's site (Magnific).

Third-party merchants may use tracking cookies to verify that a referral originated from our site. These cookies are managed by the respective merchant according to their own privacy policies.

4. Zero Data Selling

We do not sell, trade, rent, or monetize user data under any circumstances.
